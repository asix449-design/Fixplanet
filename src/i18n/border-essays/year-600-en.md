Year **600** — the world is already medieval, though nobody yet calls the word that. The Western Roman Empire has been gone over a century. In the east Rome still holds, but in two years it will be jerked so hard half the world remaps. Earth's population — about 300 million.

### Europe
- **Byzantium.** Emperor **Maurice** (582–602). He helped the Persian Khosrow II regain the throne, ended one war with Iran, and now fights in the Balkans against Avars and Slavs. He cuts the army's pay — soldiers are angry. In 602 they will overthrow and kill him with his family. That will become the pretext for a monstrous war with Persia.
- **Italy.** The **Lombards** have almost finished taking the north and center. King Agilulf. Byzantium keeps the Ravenna Exarchate, Rome, scraps of the south and the coast. Pope **Gregory the Great** (590–604) — real power in Rome: writes letters, sends missions, feeds the city. Venice is already gathering from refugees in the lagoon.
- **Franks.** The kingdom again in shreds after Clovis. Chlothar II in Neustria is weak; nephews Theudebert II and Theuderic II beat him (around Dormelles). Brunhilda still weaves intrigues. No single Gaul.
- **Spain.** Visigoths under Reccared are already Catholics (not Arians). The kingdom strengthens; the Byzantine piece in the south melts.
- **Britain.** Anglo-Saxons have almost pressed the east and center. Britons cut off: Wales, Strathclyde, Cornwall. Around this time the poem *Y Gododdin* — a lost battle at Catraeth. Kent under Æthelberht already receives Augustine's Roman mission (from 597).
- **Balkans.** Slavs settle the peninsula in earnest. Avars from Pannonia raid Thrace. Byzantium loses control of the rural interior.

### Asia
**China — Sui dynasty**  
- Emperor Wen (Yang Jian) in 581–589 gathered the country after two centuries of split. Capital Chang'an. They prepare the Grand Canal — his son Yangdi will finish it. For now this is strength: a united China again on the board. In 18 years Sui will collapse from its own mega-builds and wars; Tang will come.

**Iran**  
- Khosrow II Parviz at his peak. Sasanians rich, self-assured. Peace with Maurice. When Maurice is killed, Khosrow will go to "avenge" — and nearly devour Byzantium whole.

**India**  
- No Guptas. In the north soon Harsha (from ~606). In the south Pallavas (Mahendravarman). The political map again a mosaic.

**Steppe and Far East**  
- Turks (Turkic Khaganate) are already the main steppe force after the Rouran.  
- Korea: three states, Goguryeo strong.  
- Japan — Asuka period. Regent Shōtoku under Empress Suiko: Buddhism, Chinese model of state; in a couple of years the "Seventeen-Article Constitution."

**Arabia**  
- Still a quiet edge of tribes, caravans, and cities like Mecca. Muhammad about 30; prophecy still ahead (610). In a generation a force will come from here that breaks Persia and half of Byzantium.

### Africa and America
- Egypt and Africa still Byzantine (after Justinian).  
- Aksum Christian, but no longer the same hegemon.  
- Maya in Classic bloom. Teotihuacan leans toward decline.

### What it feels like
Year 600 — a lull before another world.

China has just become an empire again. Byzantium and Persia are still two super-giants and have not yet locked throats. Franks and Visigoths build their "Roman" kingdoms. Slavs and Lombards finish eating the antique map. In Mecca an ordinary trading year.

In two years they will kill Maurice — the last great Roman–Persian war begins. In ten years Muhammad will hear the first revelation. In twenty Sui will give way to Tang. By 650 the caliphate will run from Egypt to Iran.

Year 600 can still be described in the language of late antiquity. Year 650 — no longer. This is the last "calm" frame before the Mediterranean and Near East become unrecognizable.
