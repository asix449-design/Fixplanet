Year **1000**. For a monk in Reims this is the end of a millennium from the Nativity. Many await the end of the world — it does not come. For China this is simply another year of the Song dynasty. For a Rus man in Kiev — the twelfth year after the baptism. For an Icelander on a ship — the year when you can sail still farther west and see not Greenland, but another shore.

There is no single “world of the year 1000.” There are several large worlds that already trade and sometimes hit each other, but there is no newspaper between them.

### Europe
Holy Roman Empire. Emperor **Otto III** — young, strange, wants to revive the Rome of Charlemagne and the ancient caesars at once. In March he rides to Gniezno, to the relics of Saint Adalbert. There a meeting with the Polish duke **Bolesław the Brave**: honours, gifts, an almost royal gesture. Poland enters the “family” of Christian rulers in earnest. Then Otto in Aachen opens Charlemagne’s tomb — looks at the dead emperor as a model. Eerie and very of its time.

Around this same time the Hungarian prince **Stephen** (István) takes the royal title and crown — who exactly sent it, pope or emperor, they still argue. Magyars from steppe horsemen become a Christian kingdom. Europe in the east closes the hole through which arrows flew for a hundred years.

England. King Æthelred the Unready. Danish Vikings again go raiding. A single “English kingdom” is no longer only a bud — it exists, but fragile. Normandy opposite, across the Channel, is still a duchy, not a conqueror.

France. The Capetians sit in Paris and in practice own a scrap around it. The rest — dukes stronger than the king.

Scandinavia is forming into kingdoms. In September 1000 (or about then) at Svolder the Norwegian king Olaf Tryggvason falls: Danes and Swedes beat him. Christianity in the North is no longer news, but not yet the everyday of every fjord village. Iceland at the Althing just accepts baptism — without a sword from above, by its own decision.

Rome. Pope Sylvester II — the learned Frenchman Gerbert, Otto’s friend. Not the pope who commands kings like Innocent two hundred years later. The city is dirty, noble clans cut each other; the emperor comes to restore order and leaves.

Fear of the “year one thousand” in the chronicles is less than romantics will later write. Someone awaited Judgment. The morning of 1 January arrived. They went back to ploughing.

### Rus
Kiev. Prince **Vladimir Sviatoslavich**, already baptised Basil. The baptism of 988 is not one day and not the whole country at once. By 1000 churches stand in the towns, the tithe is set, priests come from the Greeks. In the village for a long time yet Perun and Elijah will share one shelf.

Vladimir holds a great land: from Novgorod to the steppe. He seats sons in the towns — that is both strength and future civil strife. Khazaria as an empire is already gone: his father Sviatoslav finished it off. Pechenegs at the border. With Byzantium — kinship: Anna, sister of the emperors, Vladimir’s wife. Around 1000 six thousand Russian mercenaries fight for basileus Basil II in Armenia. They pay for the bride in blood.

This is not yet Moscow and not “Russia.” This is the Kievan realm with a Varangian leaven, Slavic speech and Greek faith. The choice of faith in 988 decided that this piece of Europe would go not to Rome but to Constantinople. In 1000 that is already a fact, not an experiment.

### Byzantium and the Islamic world
Constantinople — one of three or four real world cities. Emperor Basil II, soon to be called Bulgar-Slayer: he will finish the Bulgars in fourteen years. The empire is strong again after the failures of the tenth century: Crete, Cyprus, scraps of Syria recovered.

The Islamic world in a “golden age” in science and not in unity in politics.

- Córdoba in Spain — a huge city, libraries, gardens. The Caliphate of Córdoba at its peak and already on the eve of collapse.
- Cairo — Fatimids, a Shia caliphate, rival of Baghdad.
- Baghdad — the Abbasid caliph more a shrine than a master. Real power with the emirs.

Mathematics, medicine, translation of the Greeks — here. Europe will later take this back through Spain and Sicily.

### Asia
**China, Song.** The most populous and richest country in the world. Kaifeng — hundreds of thousands of people. Paper, printing, the compass, fine porcelain, a huge river fleet. In the north the Khitans of the Liao empire. Song pays them silver for peace and thinks that reasonable. For a Chinese person Europe is a rumour at the edge.

**Japan.** Heian, Kyoto, courtly beauty; *The Tale of Genji* is about to be written. Real power is already leaking to houses like Fujiwara.

**India** is sliced up. In the south the Cholas are strong: fleet, Ceylon, influence to Southeast Asia. In the north their own kingdoms. The Muslim conquest of Delhi is still ahead.

Angkor in Cambodia — a stone forest of temples, one of the great capitals.

### The northwest ocean
Around the year 1000 **Leif Erikson**, son of Erik the Red, goes farther from the Greenland farms. The sagas name Helluland, Markland, Vinland — most likely Baffin Island, Labrador, a piece of Newfoundland. Europeans set foot on America five centuries before Columbus. A colony does not come of it: too few people, bitter clashes with locals, far from home. Later they will forget so thoroughly that Columbus will “discover” it again.

This is not the “discovery of America” in the schoolbook sense. This is a boot-print on the edge of the map that the map did not keep.

### Africa
The Ghana Empire in the Sahel — gold and salt across the Sahara. The east coast is tied to the Indian Ocean: Swahili is still forming. Fatimid Egypt — a Mediterranean power.

### What it feels like
The year 1000 — not a new world and not the end of the old. This is the year when several civilisations at once decide to which god to ascribe the crown.

Poland, Hungary, Rus, Iceland, Norway — Christianity becomes not a mission but a state. Islam has long lived that way from Córdoba to Central Asia. China lives by its own heaven and its own ancestors. Vikings at this moment both baptise and discover Vinland — a cross on a longship’s prow does not stop anyone from seeking timber beyond the ocean.

People on earth — on the order of 250–300 million. Half as if in East Asia. The biggest city in Europe after Córdoba — Constantinople. Paris is still small.

What follows:
- 1015 — death of Vladimir, strife of the sons;
- 1054 — the schism of the churches;
- 1066 — Normans take England;
- 1071 — Manzikert, Seljuks beat Byzantium;
- 1096 — the First Crusade.

1000 is the year Europe remembered as a round number. The rest of the world lived it as ordinary. The holy did not happen. Something else did: on the western edge of the Atlantic someone saw grapes and named the land Vinland. Then that place dropped out of memory for two hundred years. The map was already bigger than they thought in Rome. Rome simply did not find out.
