Year **1900**. By the calendar it is the last year of the nineteenth century. By feeling — already the twentieth: electricity, the Métro, the zeppelin, quanta. And at the same time — punitive expeditions, the siege of the legations in Beijing, farmers with rifles against the British Empire. The world is almost entirely carved up. China tries to throw the foreigners out and gets eight armies at once.

### China: the “Boxers”
In northern China for several years the **Yihetuan** movement has been growing — the “Righteous and Harmonious Fists.” Europeans call them Boxers: they practised a gymnastics like martial arts and believed amulets would stop bullets.

Why the explosion. After China’s defeat by Japan in 1895 the great powers carved themselves “spheres of influence”: ports, railways, missions, concessions. Missionaries, traders, engineers — for many Chinese this breaks the village, the temple and dignity. Droughts and famine add rage. The slogan is simple: foreigners and Christianity out.

In spring 1900 the bands burn churches, kill missionaries and Chinese Christians, tear up the rails. Empress Dowager Cixi hesitates at first, then effectively sides with them. In June they kill the German minister Ketteler. The **siege of the Beijing legation quarter** begins — about 55 days. Locked in the quarter are diplomats, guard soldiers, civilians: Russians, English, French, Americans, Japanese, Germans and others.

Against China an **Eight-Nation Alliance** gathers: Japan, Russia, Britain, France, the USA, Germany, Italy, Austria-Hungary. They take the Dagu forts, fight for Tianjin, in August take Beijing. The court flees to Xi’an. The allies loot the capital — that too is part of the story, not only the “rescue of the legations.”

Russia separately sends troops into Manchuria: there the Yihetuan strike at the Chinese Eastern Railway, which Russia built. The occupation of Manchuria will later become one of the causes of the war with Japan in 1904–1905.

Peace will be formalised in September 1901 by the “Boxer Protocol”: a huge indemnity for 39 years, executions and exile of Chinese officials, the right to keep troops at the legations, destruction of the Dagu forts. The Qing dynasty will never recover after that. The revolution of 1911 is close.

The USA in the same year pushes the “Open Door” policy: do not carve China into colonies, leave the market open to all. In practice that too is empire, only commercial.

### Africa: the Anglo-Boer War
In South Africa the **Second Anglo-Boer War** is on (since 1899). The Boers are descendants of Dutch colonists; they have two republics: the Transvaal and the Orange Free State. Under the ground — gold and diamonds. Britain wants control.

At first the Boers beat the English. Then the empire sends a huge army. In 1900 the British take the capitals and formally annex the republics. The war does not end: the Boers go into guerrilla fighting. The English answer with camps for families — many women and children die of disease. Peace will come in 1902. In eight years they will cook the Union of South Africa out of this.

The French in the same year secure Chad. The “Scramble for Africa” is almost finished: almost no “nobody’s land” left on the map.

### Europe and science
In Paris the **World’s Fair** and the Olympic Games (the second modern ones, very chaotic: sport mixed with a fair). They open the first kilometres of the Paris Métro.

On 2 July the first flight of the airship **Zeppelin LZ 1**.

The German physicist **Max Planck** writes the paper from which quantum physics begins: light and energy are emitted in packets, not as a continuous stream. At the time it looks like a formula for a stove. In a generation the atom and electronics will come from it.

**Freud** publishes *The Interpretation of Dreams*. Psychoanalysis enters culture.

In London trade unions and socialists form a committee from which in 1906 the Labour Party will be born.

Germany is already the world’s second industrial power after the USA, overtaking Britain in steel. Europe is sure of itself. Fourteen years to the world war.

### Russia
The tsar is **Nicholas II**. The empire is huge, industry grows, the Trans-Siberian is nearly joined up. But inside it already seethes: workers’ circles, students, the national borderlands.

In 1900 the main foreign business is China and Manchuria. Russian soldiers in Beijing and on the CER. It seems the Far East is “ours.” In four years Japan will contest that and win.

Toll’s polar expedition goes north. This is still an empire of explorers, not only of gendarmes.

### The Americas
President McKinley. The USA after the war with Spain (1898) already holds the Philippines, Puerto Rico, influences Cuba. A new empire without the word “empire.”

In September a hurricane destroys Galveston in Texas: six to eight thousand dead, one of the worst natural blows in US history.

They put the dollar on the gold standard. The navy takes its first modern submarine, the *Holland*.

From 1 January 1901 the Australian colonies will become one dominion — the Commonwealth of Australia. The law is passed in advance.

### What it feels like
The year 1900 — glitter and violence on one postcard.

Paris shows light and machines. Beijing burns and is looted. In the laboratory the quantum is born. In South Africa the empire learns that a farmer’s rifle and a camp for families are also “civilisation.” China gets a bill it cannot pay without humiliation.

The world is sure that progress is endless and the white man runs it. In 14 years that same Europe will kill millions of its own. In 17 the Russian monarchy will fall. In 11 — the Chinese.

What follows:
- 1901 — death of Victoria; murder of McKinley, Roosevelt becomes president; the Boxer Protocol;
- 1903 — the Wright brothers’ aeroplane;
- 1904–1905 — the Russo-Japanese War;
- 1905 — revolution in Russia;
- 1914 — the First World War.

1900 is the year when the nineteenth century threw itself a farewell fireworks show and did not notice that the fuse was already smouldering.
