Year **900** — the Viking Age is not over yet, but the great empires are crumbling. The Carolingians are already gone as a unity. The Tang are living out their last years. The caliphate in Baghdad rules more in name. On the fringes new forces gather: Wessex, the Samanids, Oleg’s Rus, the Magyars.

### Europe
- **East Franks.** They proclaim seven-year-old Louis the Child king. Archbishops and dukes rule. Charlemagne’s empire after Verdun (843) — three pieces, all weak. Magyars already fly on raids across Germany and Italy: light cavalry, tribute, fires. Venice in June beats them off at the Rialto.
- **West Franks** are in civil strife too. Flanders arranges political murders. Rollo and Normandy (911) are almost here: the Normans already sit in the lower Seine.
- **England.** Alfred the Great died in 899. On 8 June at Kingston they crown his son — **Edward the Elder**. The Danelaw is still Danish. Ahead lies the reconquest of Mercia and the east together with his sister Æthelflæd. There is no single England yet, but the vector is already clear.
- **The North.** Around this time Harald Fairhair is counted the first king of all Norway (after Hafrsfjord). Iceland is being settled. Greenland is about to be “noticed” (Gunnbjörn around 900).
- **Italy.** The papacy enters the stretch of “pornocracy.” Lombard principalities of the south live on their own. Muslims have almost finished taking Sicily (902).
- **Rus.** Oleg in Kiev (from 882). The state already exists; the route “from the Varangians to the Greeks” works. The great campaign on Tsargrad — in 907.

### Byzantium
- Emperor Leo VI the Wise. Macedonian dynasty. The empire has come alive after the nightmare of the seventh century: presses the Arabs in Cilicia, fights for Sicily and southern Italy, bargains with Simeon’s Bulgars. Constantinople is rich and learned again.

### The Islamic world
- Caliph al-Mu’tadid in Baghdad. The caliphate is still huge on the map, but real power leaks away to governors.
- Spring of 900: the Samanid Ismail ibn Ahmad beats the Saffarid Amr at Balkh. Khorasan and Transoxiana — for the Samanids. This is a Sunni Iranian renaissance: Bukhara will become brilliant.
- The Qarmatians (Ismailis) shake Iraq and Syria. The future founder of the Fatimids is already moving into North Africa.
- Córdoba — a separate Umayyad emirate, on the road to a caliphate. Egypt is still Tulunid; soon Baghdad will temporarily take it back.

### Asia
**China**  
- The Tang are dying. The emperor is a puppet; the country is run by jiedushi; the capital is yanked this way and that. In seven years Zhu Wen will overthrow the Tang — the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms begin. In the north the Khitans rise (soon the Liao empire).

**Korea**  
- Silla is crumbling. Around 900 Gyeon Hwon founds Later Baekje. Ahead: the Three Kingdoms again, then Goryeo.

**Japan**  
- Heian in full bloom. The Fujiwara already overshadow the throne. In 901 they will exile Sugawara no Michizane.

### The Americas
- The Classic Maya of the lowlands are collapsing; cities are abandoned. In the highlands life goes on. Around 900 the Toltecs come into Mexico.

### What it feels like
Year 900 — taking apart a big construction set.

Charlemagne has been dead almost a century; his empire is three tired kingdoms plus Hungarians on horseback. Baghdad still mints the caliph’s name, while Persian emirs hold the land. Chang’an is dying out. Vikings from raiders become dukes, kings and merchants on every sea — from Dublin to the Volga.

New nodes are already visible: Wessex gathers the English, Kiev gathers the Rus, the Samanids gather eastern Iran, Córdoba lives apart, Byzantium is unexpectedly in order.

In eleven years Rollo will get Normandy. In seven years the Tang will fall. In sixty-two years Otto will beat the Magyars. In a century — the Baptism of Rus and already another world.

900 is the year when the old roofs leak and the new ones still have no tiles. You can live. Quietly — no.
