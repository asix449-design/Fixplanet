Year **700** — the Islamic world is already the main power from the Atlantic almost to Central Asia. Persia is gone. Byzantium has shrunk, but it lives. China under the Tang (and under an empress) is on the rise. Europe — kingdoms and mayors of the palace.

### Near East and the Mediterranean
- **Umayyad Caliphate**, capital Damascus. Caliph Abd al-Malik (until 705). Arabic becomes the language of power; they mint their own dinars; the Dome of the Rock in Jerusalem already stands. The state is huge: Arabia, Syria, Iraq, Iran, Egypt, almost all of the Maghreb.
- In 698 Carthage is taken and destroyed — Byzantium has lost Africa for good. Nearby they build Tunis. Musa ibn Nusayr finishes off Berber resistance in the west. Gibraltar is within reach: Spain will fall in 711.
- In the east, revolts still flare (Sistan). Armenia is being squeezed; Theodosiopolis is taken.
- The Shia–Sunni split is already an accomplished fact (Karbala was in 680).

### Byzantium and the Balkans
- Emperor Tiberius III (usurper after Leontios). The “Twenty Years’ Anarchy” is underway: emperors overthrown, noses cut off; Justinian II sits in exile among the Khazars and will return yet.
- Syria, Egypt, Africa lost. What remains: Anatolia, Constantinople, scraps of Greece, the Exarchate of Ravenna, Sicily, southern Italy.
- Slavs and Avars sit on the Balkans almost to the Peloponnese. The First Bulgarian Empire already exists (from ~681). The Khazars are an important neighbour on the northern Black Sea.

### Europe
- **Franks.** The Merovingians are still “kings,” but power sits with the mayors of the palace. Pepin of Herstal gathers Austrasia and presses Neustria. Charles Martel and Poitiers (732) are not far off.
- **Italy.** Lombards: Cunipert has died; at once civil war — Liutpert, Raginpert, the throne jumps. The pope in Rome, Byzantines in Ravenna. The Lombards are mostly Catholics now, not Arians.
- **Spain.** The Visigothic kingdom still stands — and does not know that in eleven years it will be swept away.
- **Britain.** The Heptarchy: Northumbria, Mercia, Wessex and the rest. Around this time the Lindisfarne Gospels are written. Monasteries are centres of learning.

### Asia
**China — Tang**  
- Formally the Tang dynasty. In fact since 690 **Wu Zetian** rules — the only woman emperor in Chinese history. Buddhism is in favour, the court is brilliant, Chang’an is one of the greatest cities in the world.
- In the northeast in 698 the state of Balhae is founded. Korea: Silla has united the peninsula; in the north, remnants of Goguryeo/Balhae.

**India and the seas**  
- Fragmentation. On Sumatra, Srivijaya is rising — a maritime power of the straits.
- Islam is only just approaching Sindh (they will conquer it in 711–712).

**Japan**  
- Asuka period / start of Nara. The capital will soon be Fujiwara, then Heijō. Buddhism and the Chinese model of state are already serious business.

### The Americas
- The Classic Maya are still strong: at Tikal around this time Temple I (Temple of the Great Jaguar). Teotihuacan is already collapsing.

### What it feels like
Year 700 — a new centre of gravity.

The Mediterranean is no longer a “Roman lake.” The southern shore speaks Arabic. Damascus contests Constantinople for the title of capital of the world — and for now Damascus wins on territory. China is its own world, huge and confident. Europe is still provincial: kings weak, the church strong, cities small.

In eleven years the Arabs are in Spain. In eighteen — the second great siege of Constantinople. In thirty-two — Poitiers. In fifty the Umayyads will be replaced by the Abbasids; the capital will move to Baghdad.

700 is the moment when the Islamic caliphate is already an empire, Byzantium is no longer a superpower of the old kind, and China is again at a peak. The map of the seventh century has closed. The eighth is opening.
